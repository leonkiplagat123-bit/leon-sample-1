# How to Pay at a Shop with Buy Goods (Till Number)

Buy Goods is the quickest way to pay at shops, salons, restaurants, and market stalls. You enter a short till number and the amount, and you're done in seconds. In most cases it costs you nothing.

### What you need <a href="#what-you-need" id="what-you-need"></a>

* Enough M-Pesa balance to cover the purchase
* The shop's **till number** (usually 5 to 7 digits, often printed on a card or sticker at the counter)
* Your M-Pesa PIN

There is no account number with a till. If a business asks you for one, it's probably a Paybill instead.

### Option 1: Using USSD <a href="#option-1-using-ussd" id="option-1-using-ussd"></a>

1. Dial \*334# and choose Lipa na M-Pesa.
2. Select **Buy Goods and Services**.
3. Enter the till number.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and confirm.

### Option 2: Using the M-Pesa app <a href="#option-2-using-the-m-pesa-app" id="option-2-using-the-m-pesa-app"></a>

1. Open the M-Pesa app and sign in.
2. Tap **Pay**, then **Buy Goods**.
3. Enter or scan the till number.
4. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN.

### Option 3: Using the SIM Toolkit <a href="#option-3-using-the-sim-toolkit" id="option-3-using-the-sim-toolkit"></a>

1. Open the SIM Toolkit menu on your phone and choose M-Pesa.
2. Choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Buy Goods and Services.
3. Follow the same steps as USSD.

### Option 4: Pay from a prompt <a href="#option-4-pay-from-a-prompt" id="option-4-pay-from-a-prompt"></a>

Some merchants, such as restaurants and online stores, send a Buy Goods prompt straight to your phone after you give them your Safaricom number. Check that the till name and amount on the prompt are correct, then enter your PIN.

### Does it cost anything? <a href="#does-it-cost-anything" id="does-it-cost-anything"></a>

For customers, paying a Buy Goods till is free. The shop pays the fee, not you. The main exception is fuel stations, where customers may still pay a fee. Check the amount on your confirmation screen before you enter your PIN.

### Paying from Airtel Money <a href="#paying-from-airtel-money" id="paying-from-airtel-money"></a>

Airtel Money customers can also pay Safaricom tills. Choose Buy Goods, then M-Pesa Till, and enter the till number.

### After you pay <a href="#after-you-pay" id="after-you-pay"></a>

You'll get a confirmation SMS from M-Pesa. Keep it until you've received your goods. The shop will also get a notification, so ask them to confirm it before you leave.

### Common problems and fixes <a href="#common-problems-and-fixes" id="common-problems-and-fixes"></a>

* **Using Pay Bill for a till.** You'll see an "invalid business number" message. Cancel and use the Buy Goods and Services menu instead.
* **Wrong till number.** The money goes to another business. Call Safaricom on 100 immediately with your confirmation SMS.
* **No confirmation SMS.** Don't pay again straight away. Wait a minute and check your messages, because the first payment may have gone through.
* **Scam tills.** Read the till number aloud and confirm the business name on the prompt before you press send.

### Till vs Paybill at a glance <a href="#till-vs-paybill-at-a-glance" id="till-vs-paybill-at-a-glance"></a>

|                   | Buy Goods (Till)           | Paybill                            |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Numbers you enter | Till number only           | Business number and account number |
| Typical use       | Shops, salons, restaurants | Utilities, school fees, invoices   |
| Customer fee      | Usually free               | trascation fee                     |
