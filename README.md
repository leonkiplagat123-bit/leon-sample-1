# How to Send Money with M-Pesa

This guide shows you how to send money to another M-Pesa user from your phone, in about one minute.

#### Before you start

• A Safaricom SIM card registered for M-Pesa

• Your M-Pesa PIN

• Enough money in your M-Pesa account to cover the amount and the transaction fee

• The phone number of the person you're paying

#### Steps

1\. Open the M-Pesa menu on your phone. You can dial \*334# or open the M-Pesa app.

2\. Select Send Money.

3\. Enter the phone number of the person you're sending to.

4\. Enter the amount.

5\. Enter your M-Pesa PIN.

6\. Check the name and amount shown, then confirm.

7\. Wait for the confirmation SMS. It contains a transaction code. Keep it as proof of payment.

#### Troubleshooting

• "Insufficient funds": Your balance can't cover the amount plus the fee. Top up or send less.

• Wrong PIN: Re-enter it carefully. Too many wrong attempts will lock your account, so use the PIN reset option if you've forgotten it.

• Sent to the wrong number: Contact Safaricom customer care right away to request a reversal. Don't wait.

• No confirmation SMS: Check your M-Pesa messages or statement before trying again, so you don't pay twice.
