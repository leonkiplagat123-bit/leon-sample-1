# How to Pay a Bill with M-Pesa (Paybill)

Paybill lets you pay businesses like electricity, water, school fees, DStv, or your landlord straight from your phone. You don’t need to visit an office or stand in a queue.

#### What you need

* Enough M-Pesa balance to cover the bill (plus any fee)
* The **business number** (Paybill number)
* The **account number** for the bill
* Your M-Pesa PIN

Get both numbers from your bill or the company’s official website, or by asking their customer care.

#### Option 1: Using USSD

1. Dial \*334# and choose Lipa na M-Pesa. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
2. Select **Pay Bill**.
3. Enter the business number.
4. Enter the account number exactly as shown on your bill, without extra spaces or hyphens. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
5. Enter the amount.
6. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and confirm. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)

#### Option 2: Using the M-Pesa app

1. Open the M-Pesa app and sign in. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
2. Choose Pay, then Paybill. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
3. Enter the business number, account number, and amount.
4. Enter your PIN to confirm.

#### After you pay

You’ll get a confirmation SMS from M-Pesa. Keep it as proof of payment. If the company says they didn’t receive your payment, contact them with that confirmation message. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)

#### Common problems and fixes

* **Paying a Till number as a Paybill.** Till numbers are paid under Buy Goods and Services, not Pay Bill. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
* **Wrong account number.** The money reaches the company, but it isn’t matched to you. Contact the company with your confirmation SMS.
* **Wrong business number.** Call Safaricom on 100 immediately. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
* **No response after sending.** Wait a minute and check your messages before trying again, so you don’t pay twice. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)

#### Does it cost anything?

Not every Paybill charges the same way, so check the amount shown on your confirmation screen before you enter your PIN. [businessradar](https://businessradar.co.ke/?p=6614)
