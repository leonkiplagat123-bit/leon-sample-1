# How to Withdraw Cash from an M-Pesa Agent or ATM

When you need physical cash, you can withdraw from your M-Pesa at an agent or at a cardless ATM. This guide shows both ways, what they cost, and how to avoid common mistakes.

### Before you start <a href="#before-you-start" id="before-you-start"></a>

* Make sure you have enough M-Pesa balance to cover the amount **plus the withdrawal fee**.
* The minimum withdrawal at an agent is **KSh 50**.
* ATM withdrawals start at **KSh 200** and go up to **KSh 35,000** per transaction. For larger amounts, use an agent.

### Option 1: Withdraw from an M-Pesa agent <a href="#option-1-withdraw-from-an-m-pesa-agent" id="option-1-withdraw-from-an-m-pesa-agent"></a>

1. Go to an M-Pesa agent and ask for their **agent number** (also called the till or store number).
2. Dial \*334# on your Safaricom line.
3. Select **Withdraw Cash**.
4. Select **From Agent**.
5. Enter the agent number.
6. Enter the amount.
7. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and confirm.
8. Show the confirmation SMS to the agent and collect your cash.

You can also start the withdrawal from the M-Pesa app or the My Safaricom app by choosing Withdraw Cash.

### Option 2: Withdraw from an ATM (no card needed) <a href="#option-2-withdraw-from-an-atm-no-card-needed" id="option-2-withdraw-from-an-atm-no-card-needed"></a>

This works at ATMs that show the M-Pesa logo, such as those from Equity, KCB, Co-op, NCBA, and Absa.

1. Dial \*334# or open the M-Pesa app.
2. Select **Withdraw Cash**.
3. Select **From ATM**.
4. Enter the ATM number shown on the machine, then your M-Pesa PIN.
5. You'll get a withdrawal code by SMS. Use it quickly, because it expires after about 10 minutes.
6. At the ATM, press the **M-Pesa** option (you don't need a card).
7. Enter the code and the other details it asks for, then collect your cash.

### How much does it cost? <a href="#how-much-does-it-cost" id="how-much-does-it-cost"></a>

Withdrawal fees depend on the amount. Some examples from the 2026 tariff:

| Amount    | Agent fee | ATM fee |
| --------- | --------- | ------- |
| KSh 1,000 | KSh 29    |         |
| KSh 2,000 | KSh 29    | KSh 35  |
| KSh 5,000 | KSh 69    |         |

Agent fees can climb to about KSh 309 for the largest amounts, and every fee includes 20% excise duty. For small amounts an agent is usually cheaper, while for bigger amounts the ATM can be cheaper in some bands. Confirm the exact fee on your screen before you enter your PIN.

### Tips to save money <a href="#tips-to-save-money" id="tips-to-save-money"></a>

* **Pay with M-Pesa instead of cash.** Paying a shop through Buy Goods is free for you, so you skip the withdrawal fee entirely.
* **Withdraw less often, in larger amounts**, when it makes sense for your budget.
* **Check the fee band.** A small difference in the amount can change the fee. For example, KSh 2,500 costs KSh 29 at an agent, but KSh 2,600 costs KSh 52.

### Common problems and fixes <a href="#common-problems-and-fixes" id="common-problems-and-fixes"></a>

* **The agent says they have no float.** Withdraw a smaller amount or try another agent.
* **Wrong agent number.** Cancel and try again, and double-check the number with the agent before you send. Withdrawals can't normally be reversed once you have the cash.
* **ATM code entered wrongly.** After three wrong attempts the ATM cancels the transaction, so you'll need to request a new code.
* **The code expired.** Generate a new one from the Withdraw Cash menu.
*   **You can't deposit into someone else's account at an agent.** Agents only deposit into your own account. To give someone money, use Send Money.\

### Stay safe <a href="#stay-safe" id="stay-safe"></a>

* Never share your PIN or withdrawal code with anyone, including people who claim to be Safaricom staff.
* Check the confirmation message with the agent before you leave.
* Count your cash before you walk away.
