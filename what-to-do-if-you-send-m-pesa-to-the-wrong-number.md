# What to Do If You Send M-Pesa to the Wrong Number

It happens to almost everyone: one wrong digit and your money lands in a stranger's wallet. Don't panic. The most important thing is to **act immediately**, because once the wrong recipient withdraws or spends the money, it becomes very hard to recover.

### Step 1: Keep the confirmation message <a href="#step-1-keep-the-confirmation-message" id="step-1-keep-the-confirmation-message"></a>

Don't delete the M-Pesa SMS you received after sending the money. It contains the transaction code, amount, date, time, and the number you sent to. You'll need all of it.

### Step 2: Request a reversal by SMS (fastest) <a href="#step-2-request-a-reversal-by-sms-fastest" id="step-2-request-a-reversal-by-sms-fastest"></a>

1. Copy the **entire** original M-Pesa confirmation message.
2. Send it as an SMS to **456**.
3. Wait for Safaricom's update. You should be told that the reversal has started, with a further update within about two hours.

This self-service option works best when you do it **within 24 hours** of the transaction. It applies to Safaricom-to-Safaricom transfers and to airtime bought for someone else.

### Step 3: Call Safaricom if the SMS option doesn't work <a href="#step-3-call-safaricom-if-the-sms-option-doesnt-work" id="step-3-call-safaricom-if-the-sms-option-doesnt-work"></a>

1. Dial **100** and choose the option to speak to a customer care agent.
2. Answer the identity questions the agent asks.
3. Give the agent the number you sent money to by mistake, the amount, and the transaction code.
4. The agent will start the reversal for you.

If you're outside Kenya, you can use the Help and Reversal option in the M-Pesa app, or contact Safaricom customer care by phone, email, or social media. In an email, include the transaction ID, amount, date, and the incorrect recipient's number.

### Step 4: Contact the recipient politely <a href="#step-4-contact-the-recipient-politely" id="step-4-contact-the-recipient-politely"></a>

If you can, call or message the person who received the money. Explain that you sent it by mistake, give them the transaction details, and ask them to return it. Be polite but clear, and give a reasonable deadline.

### Step 5: If the recipient refuses <a href="#step-5-if-the-recipient-refuses" id="step-5-if-the-recipient-refuses"></a>

The recipient can decline a reversal, and Safaricom cannot force it. If this happens:

1. Save the SMS saying the reversal was declined, plus the original transaction message and any messages between you and the recipient.
2. Report the matter to the police and ask for an **Occurrence Book (OB) number**.
3. Contact Safaricom again with the OB number. The police can liaise with Safaricom to help trace the transaction.

Keeping money that was sent by mistake may be treated as an offence under Kenyan law, so a police report carries weight.

### What can't be reversed <a href="#what-cant-be-reversed" id="what-cant-be-reversed"></a>

* Money the wrong recipient has already withdrawn or spent
* Airtime purchases made for yourself
* Transfers from a bank account to an M-Pesa number (contact your bank instead)
* Disputed merchant payments, where Safaricom can assist after escalation but cannot reverse on its own

Report any wrong transaction as early as possible. Automated reversals by SMS or the app are only available within 24 hours, and Safaricom advises reporting within one month at most.

### How to avoid sending to the wrong number <a href="#how-to-avoid-sending-to-the-wrong-number" id="how-to-avoid-sending-to-the-wrong-number"></a>

* Use **Hakikisha**, which shows the recipient's registered name before you confirm.
* Check the number, the name, and the amount before entering your PIN.
* Save frequent contacts instead of typing numbers each time.
* For large amounts, send a small test payment first.

### Quick checklist <a href="#quick-checklist" id="quick-checklist"></a>

| Time             | What to do                                                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Immediately      | Keep the SMS and forward it to 456                                    |
| Within 24 hours  | Follow up on the reversal and call 100 if needed                      |
| If declined      | Contact the recipient, then report to the police and get an OB number |
| Within one month | Last window to report the transaction to Safaricom                    |
