# How Fuliza charges you

There are two main costs:

1. **Access fee:** a one-off fee of 1% of the amount you borrow, charged when you borrow.
2. **Daily maintenance fee:** charged every midnight on the amount you still owe, starting the day after you borrow. Safaricom adds **20% excise duty** to this fee.

| Amount you owe (KSh) | Daily fee | Excise (20%) | Total per day |
| -------------------- | --------- | ------------ | ------------- |
| 1 to 100             | 0         | 0            | 0             |
| 101 to 500           | 2.50      | 0.50         | 3             |
| 501 to 1,000         | 5         | 1            | 6             |
| 1,001 to 1,500       | 18        | 3.60         | 21.60         |
| 1,501 to 2,500       | 20        | 4            | 24            |
| 2,501 to 70,000      | 25        | 5            | 30            |

Borrowing KSh 1,000 or less has **three free days** for the daily fee. If you repay within three days, you only pay the 1% access fee.

#### Examples

* Borrow KSh 1,000 and repay within three days: access fee only, which is **KSh 10**.
* Borrow KSh 5,000 and repay after 7 days: KSh 50 access fee, plus KSh 175 daily fees (25 x 7), plus KSh 35 excise, which is **KSh 260** in total.

_Fees last checked on 28 October 2026. Safaricom can change them, so confirm the amount on your confirmation screen._
