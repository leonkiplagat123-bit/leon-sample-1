# How Fuliza M-Pesa Works (and What It Costs)

Fuliza is an overdraft on your M-Pesa. When you don't have enough money to complete a payment, Fuliza tops up the difference so the transaction still goes through. It's convenient in an emergency, but it is a loan, and it costs money every day it stays unpaid.

### What is Fuliza? <a href="#what-is-fuliza" id="what-is-fuliza"></a>

Fuliza is provided by Safaricom together with partner banks NCBA and KCB. Each mobile number that qualifies is given a **limit**, which is the most you can borrow at any one time. You can use Fuliza as many times as you like, as long as you stay within your limit.

### Where does Fuliza work? <a href="#where-does-fuliza-work" id="where-does-fuliza-work"></a>

Fuliza only kicks in when your M-Pesa balance is too low for a transaction. It can help you with:

* Paying bills (Paybill)
* Paying at shops (Buy Goods tills)
* Sending money
* Buying airtime

### How to opt in <a href="#how-to-opt-in" id="how-to-opt-in"></a>

1. Dial \*334# on your Safaricom line.
2. Select **Loans and Savings**.
3. Select **Fuliza** and choose to opt in.

You can also opt in through the My Safaricom app. Not every number gets a limit. Safaricom sets it based on how actively you use M-Pesa and Safaricom services.

### How Fuliza charges you <a href="#how-fuliza-charges-you" id="how-fuliza-charges-you"></a>

There are two main costs:

1. **Access fee:** a one-off fee of 1% of the amount you borrow, charged when you borrow.
2. **Daily maintenance fee:** a fee charged every day on the amount you still owe, starting from midnight the day after you borrow.

On top of that, **20% excise duty** is charged on the daily maintenance fee. The lowest borrowing band (KSh 1 to 100) has no daily fee, and some small balances get a few free days before the daily fee starts. The daily fee rises with the amount you owe, so larger balances cost more per day.

Safaricom adjusted these daily fees during 2026, so always check the current table before you publish or borrow.

#### Example <a href="#example" id="example"></a>

If you use KSh 1,000 of Fuliza, the access fee is 1% of KSh 1,000, which is **KSh 10**. After that, the daily maintenance fee applies for every day the balance stays unpaid, plus excise duty on that daily fee.

### How to repay Fuliza <a href="#how-to-repay-fuliza" id="how-to-repay-fuliza"></a>

There is no separate repayment menu to remember. Any money that arrives in your M-Pesa account, whether it's received from a friend or deposited at an agent, **automatically goes to clearing your Fuliza balance first**. The remainder stays in your wallet.

Because the daily fee keeps running until you repay, clearing the balance quickly saves you more than almost anything else.

### How to grow your limit <a href="#how-to-grow-your-limit" id="how-to-grow-your-limit"></a>

* Use M-Pesa and Safaricom services regularly
* Repay on time

### How to opt out <a href="#how-to-opt-out" id="how-to-opt-out"></a>

1. Open the My Safaricom app and go to M-Pesa, then Fuliza.
2. Choose **Opt Out**.

You can also opt out through the USSD menu. Repay any outstanding balance first.

### Tips for using Fuliza wisely <a href="#tips-for-using-fuliza-wisely" id="tips-for-using-fuliza-wisely"></a>

* Treat it as an emergency tool, not extra income.
* Repay as soon as money comes in, because the daily fee adds up.
* Check your balance before every payment so you know whether Fuliza will be used.
* Remember that it is a loan from a bank partner, so unpaid balances can affect your credit record.
* Compare it with other options such as M-Shwari before borrowing larger amounts.
