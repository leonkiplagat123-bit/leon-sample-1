# How to Check Your M-Pesa Balance and Get a Statement

Knowing your balance and keeping a record of your transactions helps you budget, avoid failed payments, and prove that you paid someone. Both are free, and you can do them from any phone.

### Part 1: Check your M-Pesa balance <a href="#part-1-check-your-m-pesa-balance" id="part-1-check-your-m-pesa-balance"></a>

#### Option 1: Using USSD (no internet needed) <a href="#option-1-using-ussd-no-internet-needed" id="option-1-using-ussd-no-internet-needed"></a>

1. Dial \*334# on your Safaricom line.
2. Select **My Account**.
3. Select **Check Balance**.
4. Enter your M-Pesa PIN if asked.
5. Your balance is sent to you by SMS.

#### Option 2: Using the M-Pesa app <a href="#option-2-using-the-m-pesa-app" id="option-2-using-the-m-pesa-app"></a>

Open the M-Pesa app. Your balance shows on the home tab. If you turned on **hide balance**, tap it and enter your PIN to reveal it.

#### Option 3: Using the SIM Toolkit <a href="#option-3-using-the-sim-toolkit" id="option-3-using-the-sim-toolkit"></a>

1. Open the SIM Toolkit menu on your phone and select **M-Pesa**.
2. Select **My Account**, then **Check Balance**.
3. Enter your M-Pesa PIN.
4. Wait for the SMS with your balance.

#### Does it cost anything? <a href="#does-it-cost-anything" id="does-it-cost-anything"></a>

No. Checking your balance is free, and you can do it as many times as you like.

### Part 2: Get your M-Pesa statement <a href="#part-2-get-your-m-pesa-statement" id="part-2-get-your-m-pesa-statement"></a>

There are two kinds of statement:

| Type           | What you get                                | How it arrives  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Mini statement | Your last few transactions                  | SMS             |
| Full statement | A complete record for the period you choose | Email, as a PDF |

#### Option 1: Mini statement <a href="#option-1-mini-statement" id="option-1-mini-statement"></a>

1. Dial \*334#.
2. Select **My Account**, then **M-Pesa Statement**.
3. Choose **Mini Statement**.
4. You'll receive an SMS with your recent transactions.

You can also find the mini statement in the SIM Toolkit menu.

#### Option 2: Full statement by USSD <a href="#option-2-full-statement-by-ussd" id="option-2-full-statement-by-ussd"></a>

1. Dial \*334#.
2. Select **My Account**, then **M-Pesa Statement**.
3. Choose **Full Statement** (or Request Statement).
4. Choose the period you want.
5. Enter the email address where it should be sent.
6. The statement arrives as a PDF in your email.

#### Option 3: Statement from the app <a href="#option-3-statement-from-the-app" id="option-3-statement-from-the-app"></a>

1. Open the M-Pesa app (or the My Safaricom app).
2. Go to **Statements**.
3. Choose the period.
4. The app creates the statement for you to view or share.

#### Does it cost anything? <a href="#does-it-cost-anything-1" id="does-it-cost-anything-1"></a>

No. Requesting your own mini or full statement by USSD or in the app is free.

### Common problems and fixes <a href="#common-problems-and-fixes" id="common-problems-and-fixes"></a>

* **The statement PDF won't open.** It may be password protected. If you set a custom statement password with Safaricom, use that.
* **The statement didn't arrive.** Check your spam folder and confirm you typed your email address correctly, then request it again.
* **No internet.** USSD works without data. A mini statement comes by SMS, but you need email access to open a full statement.
* **Not seeing your balance on the app.** Check whether hide balance is switched on.
* **PIN locked.** Five wrong PIN attempts in a row lock your account. Use the M-Pesa PIN Manager under \*334# to unlock or reset it.

### Why keep your statement? <a href="#why-keep-your-statement" id="why-keep-your-statement"></a>

A statement is proof of payment, helps you track where your money goes, and is often required for loan applications and visa or tenancy applications. Request one regularly and save it somewhere safe.
