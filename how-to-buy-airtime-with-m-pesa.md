# How to Buy Airtime with M-Pesa

This guide shows you how to buy airtime for your own Safaricom number, or for someone else's, using M-Pesa in about one minute.

### Before you start

* A Safaricom SIM card registered for M-Pesa
* Your M-Pesa PIN
* Enough money in your M-Pesa account to cover the airtime amount
* The phone number you want to top up, if it's not your own

### Buy airtime for your own number

1. Dial **\*334#** or open the **M-Pesa app**.
2. Select **Buy Airtime**.
3. Choose **My number**.
4. Enter the **amount** you want to buy.
5. Enter your **M-Pesa PIN**.
6. Check the details on the screen, then confirm.
7. Wait for the **confirmation SMS**. It shows the amount, the new airtime balance, and a transaction code.

### Buy airtime for another person

1. Dial **\*334#** or open the **M-Pesa app**.
2. Select **Buy Airtime**.
3. Choose **Other number**.
4. Enter the **phone number** of the person you're topping up.
5. Enter the **amount**.
6. Enter your **M-Pesa PIN** and confirm.
7. Keep the confirmation SMS as proof. Ask the other person to check that the airtime arrived.

### Troubleshooting

* **"Insufficient funds":** Your M-Pesa balance is lower than the amount you entered. Top up your M-Pesa or enter a smaller amount.
* **Wrong number entered:** Check the number on the confirmation screen before you enter your PIN. If you bought airtime for the wrong number, contact Safaricom customer care straight away.
* **No confirmation SMS:** Check your M-Pesa messages or statement before trying again, so you don't pay twice.
* **Wrong PIN:** Re-enter it carefully. After too many wrong attempts your account may lock, so use the PIN reset option if you've forgotten it.
